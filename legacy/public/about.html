Our Story

Building a Greener Moldova

Founded in 2016, BTC Solar began with a simple mission: to make sustainable energy accessible to every household and business in Moldova. What started as a small team of passionate engineers has grown into the country's most trusted solar energy provider.

We recognized early on that Moldova has exceptional potential for solar energy, with over 280 sunny days per year. Our founders saw an opportunity to help families and businesses reduce their energy costs while contributing to environmental sustainability.

Today, we've installed over 10 megawatts of solar capacity across more than 500 projects. From small residential rooftops to large commercial installations, every project receives the same level of dedication, quality, and professional service.

Our Mission

To accelerate Moldova's transition to clean energy by providing premium solar solutions that are accessible, reliable, and deliver exceptional value. We believe every home and business deserves the power to generate their own clean energy.

Our Vision

A Moldova powered by clean, renewable energy where every community has energy independence and a reduced carbon footprint. We envision solar panels on every suitable rooftop, contributing to a sustainable future for generations to come.
